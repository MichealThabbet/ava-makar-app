# Design system — «Old Coptic Orthodox Monastery — Luxury Digital Manuscript Library»

Owner's brief, 2026-11-03 (DEC-P0-27). Code: `app/lib/design/`.

## Tokens (`tokens.dart`)
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian | #14110F | App background, hero shade |
| Walnut | #302018 | Panels, bottom bar (texture at low opacity) |
| Leather | #513329 | Covers, cards (texture) |
| Antique gold | #B68D48 | Emphasis, active state, ornaments |
| Gold highlight | #E3CCA0 | Gilt edge, active labels on dark |
| Parchment | #EFE4CF | Reading surface |
| Burgundy | #6B2429 | Secondary accent on parchment |
Gold text is used only on dark backgrounds; on parchment the text is ink #2A1E17 / #5E4A3C.

Fonts (all OFL, bundled with licences in `app/assets/fonts/`): Amiri (headings, reading), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (interface), Noto Sans Coptic (Coptic text).

## Materials (`app/assets/textures/`)
Procedural and tileable, generated by `tools/textures/generate.py` (fixed seeds): leather, walnut, sandstone (generic ashlar masonry), parchment. Aged paper sheets (`paper_1..3.png`, torn scorched edges, baked soft shadow, no frame) from `tools/textures/paper.py`; used for the home «explore» tiles with dark-brown writing (owner's request 2026-11-03). No photographs; stock paper images sent as references are not shipped (licence unknown).

## Components
`LeatherPanel`, `GoldButton` (one sheen on press), `GhostButton`, `Pressable` (0.97 press), `Reveal` (staggered fade), `Tag`, `SectionHeader`, `WalnutBackdrop`, `BookCover` / `BookTile` (Hero transition to the detail page), ornaments `CornerOrnament`, `OrnamentDivider`, `MonasteryMark`, `ArchFramePainter`. Ornaments are new designs inspired by Coptic interlace, not reproductions of documented artefacts. All motion stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Hero photograph (pending)
No licensed photograph of St Macarius Monastery is available in the project files, and none is invented or borrowed from another monastery. The hero shows a stone composition until the monastery supplies a photo. To add it: put the image in `app/assets/images/`, record source and licence in `docs/content/INVENTORY.md`, and swap the `Image.asset(Textures.sandstone …)` line in `screens/home.dart`.

## Logo
`MonasteryMark` is a placeholder (small cross in a gilt ring) until the monastery's official emblem is supplied.
