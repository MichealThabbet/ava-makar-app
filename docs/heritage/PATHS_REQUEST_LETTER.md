# Letter to the PAThs project (Prof. Paola Buzi): draft

**To:** paola.buzi@uniroma1.it
**Subject:** Monastery of St Macarius the Great (Dayr Anba Maqar): request for PAThs API access and scholarly cooperation

---

Dear Professor Buzi,

I write on behalf of the Monastery of St Macarius the Great (Dayr Anba Maqar) in Wadi al-Natrun, Egypt. I am the monastery's delegated representative for its digital heritage project. The monastery's letter of authorisation is attached.

The monastery is building a non-commercial digital archive of the manuscripts that once belonged to its library and to the monasteries of Scetis. These manuscripts are now held in the Vatican, London, Paris, Oxford and elsewhere. Your work on the literary manuscripts of Dayr Anba Maqar, including *Detecting Early Mediaeval Coptic Literature in Dayr al-Anbā Maqār* (2019), is central to this effort.

We would be grateful for:

1. **An API key** for the PAThs BraDypUS API (application `paths`). We would use it to read the records for manuscripts, works and places linked to Dayr Anba Maqar and Scetis, under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence, with full citation of PAThs.
2. **Your guidance** on the dispersed codices of Dayr Anba Maqar: which shelfmarks belong together, and where leaves of the same codex are now kept.
3. **A possible cooperation** between the monastery and the PAThs team, for example on manuscripts still in the monastery's library.

We will cite PAThs in every use, follow the licence, and share back any corrections we find.

## Notes for the owner (do not send)

- **What PAThs is.** PAThs holds data about manuscripts (where they came from, where they are now, what they contain), not images. Its data licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **Why we need a key.** Bulk access through the API needs a key that the project team issues. The atlas website itself can be browsed freely by hand.
- **We will not use a key taken from the site's code.** Using credentials found in someone else's code is against our rules (AGENTS.md / HANDOFF §10).
- **Why Prof. Buzi matters.** She is the editor of the main study of Dayr Anba Maqar's literary manuscripts. A cooperation with her is worth more than any tool.
- **Free without a key:** the project's full bibliography on Zotero: https://www.zotero.org/groups/2189557/erc-paths/
