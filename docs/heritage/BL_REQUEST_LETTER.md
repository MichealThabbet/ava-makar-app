# Letter to the British Library: request for digital copies (draft)

**To:** businessdevelopment@bl.uk
**Subject:** Request for digital copies of Coptic manuscripts for the archive of the Monastery of St Macarius the Great (Dayr Anba Maqar), Wadi al-Natrun, Egypt

---

Dear Sir or Madam,

I write on behalf of the Monastery of St Macarius the Great (Dayr Anba Maqar) in Wadi al-Natrun (Scetis), Egypt. I am the monastery's delegated representative for its digital heritage project. The monastery's letter of authorisation is attached.

Several manuscripts now in the British Library were written in, or are closely linked to, the monasteries of Scetis and the Coptic Church. The monastery is building a non-commercial study archive of its own heritage for its monks and for scholars. We would be grateful for digital copies of the manuscripts below, from existing surrogates (digitised images or microfilm), for archival and study use within the monastery.

| Shelfmark | Description (from the BL catalogue / Crum 1905) |
|---|---|
| Or 8812 | Catena on the Gospels, Bohairic, AD 888/889, from the Monastery of St Macarius |
| Or 1316 | Four Gospels, Coptic-Arabic, AD 1663 |
| Or 1317 | Companion volume to Or 1316 |
| Or 7594 | Deuteronomy, Jonah and Acts, Sahidic papyrus codex |
| Or 6801 | Miscellany in honour of St Mercurius, AD 996–1004 |
| Or 7029 | Miscellany including the Life of Aaron of Philae, 10th century |

We would also welcome:

1. Your price list, and the terms for a religious community's non-commercial archival use.
2. Whether a reduced fee or a partnership is possible for the monastery from which some of these manuscripts came.
3. Guidance on the Syriac manuscripts from the Monastery of the Syrians (Dayr al-Suryan) in Wadi al-Natrun (the Nitrian collection), with a view to a later request.

We will respect every condition of use you set. The images will not be republished without your written permission.

With thanks and respect,

[Name]
Delegated representative, Monastery of St Macarius the Great, Wadi al-Natrun, Egypt
[Email] · [Telephone]

Attachment: the monastery's letter of authorisation.

## Notes for the owner (do not send)

- **The British Library does not scan originals.** It supplies copies only from existing surrogates (earlier digitisation or microfilm). That is why the letter asks for "existing surrogates".
- **Payment** is by card, and an order is processed within about 30 working days of payment.
- **Before sending:**
  - Attach the monastery's letter of authorisation.
  - Fill in the name, email and telephone.
  - Send it from your own email or the monastery's email.
- **Next steps:** once they reply with prices, decide on the list together. Crum's catalogue (1905) is in the downloader as `catalogueofcopti00brituoft`; we use it to choose the next shelfmarks.
- **Unconfirmed points:**
  - The St Macarius provenance of Or 8812 comes from a research article (COMSt bulletin 4-2), not the BL catalogue.
  - Or 1317 is named as Or 1316's companion in a BL blog post; its catalogue record has not been checked.
